## Brand & Style

This design system channels the spirit of 1970s underground press, punk mimeograph zines, and tactile print counterculture, disciplined through the precision of contemporary swiss-editorial typography. The brand personality is unapologetic, literate, observational, and raw yet meticulously structured. It communicates the warmth and friction of ink-on-paper, rejecting sterile tech corporate minimalism without sacrificing pristine reading ergonomics for long-form essays and marginalia.

The target audience comprises independent thinkers, cultural essayists, crate-diggers, developers, and obsessive readers who appreciate tangible graphic heritage alongside immaculate web typography. Visually, the experience evokes opening a preserved broadsheet or silkscreened literary foldout discovered in an independent bookstore basement: high tactile contrast, razor-sharp rules, energetic printed stickers, and generous white (newsprint) space that lets text breathe with authority.

## Colors

The palette simulates the natural materiality of unbleached newsprint stock drenched in dense carbon ink, accented with high-impact screenprinting pigments.

- **Canvas & Paper**: The baseline background is warm unbleached newsprint (`#FBF9F5`), with secondary container fills stepping down to deeper fibrous cream (`#F4EFE6`) and aged cardstock (`#ECE5D8`).
- **Ink & Body**: Deep carbon ink (`#18181B`) serves as the foundational text and structural line color. Subdued typography uses worn toner charcoal (`#52525B`) and stamp grey (`#71717A`).
- **Accent Primaries**: A blistering counterculture signal orange (`#F95721`) is the primary accent, used deliberately for hyperlinks, active states, drop-caps, and press-mark highlights. A warm street amber (`#D97706`) functions as a secondary accent for categorization and secondary badges.
- **Rule & Border Tint**: Borders use muted sepia grit (`#E5DFD5` and `#D1C7B7`), ensuring borders resemble ruled mechanical paste-up guidelines rather than sterile digital dividers.
- **Dark Mode / Inverted Reading**: When inverted, the dark palette is rooted in dry asphalt charcoal (`#141416`), ink cream typography (`#F4EFE6`), and fluorescent vermilion orange (`#FF6E3A`) to maintain authentic high-contrast low-fatigue legibility.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy balances expressive 1970s street poster punch with effortless editorial immersion:

- **Headlines & Display (`Bricolage Grotesque`)**: Provides raw, eccentric, compressed personality with optical flair reminiscent of 70s protest headlines and independent broadsheet titles. Tightly tracked and high-density.
- **Long-Form Body (`Literata`)**: Engineered specifically for high-comfort long-form screen reading. Its humanist proportions, organic terminals, and generous vertical metric rhythm maintain optical clarity across essays, criticism, and journal entries.
- **Labels, Metadata & Marginalia (`Space Mono`)**: Represents the mechanical typewriter and teletype heritage of underground publishers. Used exclusively in uppercase or tabular styles for timestamps, reading lengths, footnoting, issue numbers, and sticker tags.
- **Editorial Flourishes**: Blockquotes use italicized `Literata` stepped up 1.25x with hanging quotation marks. Large initial drop-caps feature `Bricolage Grotesque` set in primary orange.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model adapts Swiss grid discipline to the zine's paste-up heritage:

- **Editorial Reading Column**: The primary reading column is strictly locked to `container-narrow` (max 680px) to guarantee optimal 65–75 character line lengths for long-form essays.
- **Broadsheet Grid**: Archive, catalog, and dashboard layouts leverage a modular 12-column grid inside `container-standard` (1040px) or `container-wide` (1280px) with 24px column gutters and crisp mechanical divider rules between cells.
- **Asymmetrical Asides (Marginalia)**: On screens wider than 1200px, secondary footnotes, tape annotations, and metadata break outside the primary 680px prose column into a dedicated 240px right rail.
- **Vertical Rhythm**: Built on an absolute 4px/8px base increment. Paragraph blocks enforce a consistent 20px bottom margin; major section breaks deploy physical separator rules bordered by `space-3xl` (4.5rem) vertical padding.
- **Breakpoints**:
  - `Mobile` (< 640px): Single-column paste-up flow, 20px horizontal margins, flush edge-to-edge rule dividers.
  - `Tablet` (640px – 1024px): 8-column layout, metadata docked above prose headers.
  - `Desktop` (> 1024px): Full broadsheet grid with dedicated marginalia columns and sticky navigation headers.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system deliberately rejects soft ambient blur shadows and translucent frosted glass. Depth is conveyed strictly through tactile physical print metaphors, bold outlines, and hard-edged offset drop shadows:

- **Hard-Edged Cutout Shadows**: Elevated interactive elements (cards on hover, featured stickers, active toggles) project a crisp 100% solid offset shadow (`2px 2px 0px #18181B` or `4px 4px 0px #18181B`). No blur radius is ever applied.
- **Layered Paper Tones**: Stacking depth is communicated through distinct surface paper values:
  - Base document layer: `#FBF9F5` (newsprint cream)
  - Card/Module background: `#F4EFE6` (cardstock)
  - Overlaid inspection sheets & callouts: `#ECE5D8` (unbleached kraft)
- **Mechanical Borders**: Crisp 1px and 2px borders in `#18181B` or `#E5DFD5` frame elements like cut-and-paste blocks. Internal dividers within cards use subtle hairline dashed rules (`1px dashed #D1C7B7`) reminiscent of perforation lines.

## Shapes

The design system is unapologetically sharp (`roundedness: 0`). Elements evoke guillotined paper edges, paste-up galley slips, and trimmed film stock.

- **Zero Radius (Default)**: All primary containers, image wrappers, buttons, input fields, cards, and dialogue frames have `border-radius: 0px`.
- **Rotated Sticker Exceptions**: Micro-badges, issue tags, and counterculture stamps may apply a slight deliberate tilt (`transform: rotate(-1.5deg)` or `rotate(1deg)`) to emulate hand-pasted stickers and screenprinted labels without rounding their corners.
- **Perforated Edges**: Specialized visual dividers can utilize saw-tooth or dash-spaced SVG mask patterns along lower margins to simulate ripped notebook slips or stamp sheets.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Sharp rectangular container, background carbon ink (`#18181B`), text unbleached newsprint (`#FBF9F5`), 1px solid `#18181B`. High-contrast, all-caps `Space Mono` typography. Hover: shifts background to primary signal orange (`#F95721`) with a sharp hard-edge shadow (`3px 3px 0px #18181B`). Active: transforms `translate(2px, 2px)` with shadow collapsing to `1px 1px 0px #18181B`.
- **Secondary (Paper Outlined)**: Background transparent or `#F4EFE6`, 1.5px solid carbon ink (`#18181B`), text `#18181B`. Hover: solid tint fill `#ECE5D8` with `3px 3px 0px #F95721`.
- **Tertiary / Text Button**: Underlined with a heavy 2px orange baseline offset by 4px (`text-decoration-color: #F95721`).

### Chips & Stickers
- **Issue Badges / Category Chips**: Sharp rectilinear boxes with `label-md` uppercase monospace text. Border: 1px solid `#18181B`. Background: `#FFB300` or `#F4EFE6`.
- **Tactile Sticker Variant**: Background signal orange (`#F95721`), text white (`#FFFFFF`), rotated `-1.5deg`, solid drop shadow (`2px 2px 0px #18181B`). Padding: 2px 8px.

### Cards
- **Article & Essay Cards**: Encased in 1px solid sepia border (`#E5DFD5`) over `#F4EFE6` cardstock. Includes an upper metadata row (`date // read time` in `Space Mono`), followed by a bold `Bricolage Grotesque` heading. Hover state applies an exterior 1.5px `#18181B` border and lifts with a `4px 4px 0px #18181B` solid offset.
- **Pinned / Lead Card**: Features a thick 3px solid ink top rule (`#18181B`) and a warm tinted background fill (`#ECE5D8`).

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs**: Flat zero-radius box, 1.5px solid `#18181B`, background `#FBF9F5`, text `#18181B`, font `Literata`. Placeholder text in `#71717A` with monospace prompts. Focus state: ring-less, border shifts to 2px solid `#F95721` with an interior or exterior 2px hard shadow.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: 16px square boxes with sharp corners. Unchecked: 1.5px solid `#18181B`, background transparent. Checked: solid `#18181B` fill with an interior white cutout check mark or sharp centered square pip.

### Lists & Marginalia
- **Article List**: Compact broadsheet rows separated by hairline rules (`#E5DFD5`). Monospace publication indices (e.g., `[01]`, `[02]`) in primary orange aligned left, followed by headline and trailing metadata on the far right.
- **Pull Quotes & Callouts**: Left-bordered with a heavy 4px solid stripe (`#F95721`), background `#F4EFE6`, padding 1.5rem. Set in large italic `Literata` with accompanying teletype attribution.
- **Footnotes & Asides**: Small monospace indicators (`[†]`, `[1]`) that trigger crisp paper popovers framed by `1px solid #18181B` with solid offset shadows.
